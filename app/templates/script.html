🎙️ Personal Finance Advisor Bot

5-Minute Video Presentation Script (Mobile Teleprompter)

⏱️ 0:00 - 0:45 (45s)
1. Introduction & The Problem
🖥️ On Screen: Title slide with project name, tech stack logos (Flask, Python, AI, SQLite).
"Hello everyone! Welcome to my demonstration of the AI-Powered Personal Finance Advisor Bot.

In today's fast-paced world, managing personal finances is a common challenge. Most traditional budgeting apps act as passive digital ledger sheets—they record your numbers, but they don't give you personalized, actionable advice on how to improve your financial habits.

To solve this, I designed and developed an intelligent web platform that combines full-stack web development with Generative AI. Not only does it track your income and expenses in real-time, but it also functions as your personal 24/7 financial planner—analyzing your spending habits, detecting overspending patterns, and generating customized budget and savings strategies."
⏱️ 0:45 - 1:30 (45s)
🖥️ On Screen: Show Architecture Diagram (User ➔ Flask App ➔ Database ➔ AI Engine).
🖼️ Architecture Diagram (Flask + Gemini / Groq + DB)
"Let's take a quick look at the technical architecture behind the application:

• Backend: Built on Python using the Flask web framework, structured using modular blueprints for Authentication, Financial Tracking, and AI Services.
• Database & ORM: Powered by SQLAlchemy with an SQLite database, managing structured relational models for Users, Transactions, and Budgets.
• Security: Features secure session management via Flask-Login, industry-standard password hashing using Bcrypt, and secure credential handling via environment variables.
• Frontend: Responsive and modern UI built with Jinja2 templates, Bootstrap 5, and dynamic markdown parsing with Marked.js.
• AI Engine: Integrated with Generative AI LLM APIs to perform real-time financial health evaluation and budget generation."
⏱️ 1:30 - 2:45 (75s)
3. Live Demo: Auth & Financial Tracking
🖥️ On Screen: Browser at http://127.0.0.1:5000. Register/login, then show Dashboard & add transaction.
"Now let’s jump straight into the live application.

(Action: Click Register/Login)
First, we have a complete authentication system with input validation, ensuring that each user’s financial records remain private and secure.

(Action: Show the Dashboard)
Once logged in, the user lands on the primary financial dashboard. Right at the top, clean summary cards show Total Income, Total Expenses, and the current Net Balance in Indian Rupees (₹).

(Action: Navigate to 'Add Transaction' and submit an entry)
Users can easily record their transactions. Let's add an expense—say, ₹4,500 under 'Groceries'. We click save, and it instantly updates our transaction ledger and recalculates our balances across the entire app."
⏱️ 2:45 - 4:00 (75s)
4. Core Highlight: AI Financial Advisor
🖥️ On Screen: Click 'AI Advisor' tab in navbar. Scroll down to show generated tables & recommendations.
"Here is where the real power of the platform comes alive: the AI Financial Advisor.

Instead of giving generic advice, our backend aggregates the user's live transaction history, categorizes their expenditures, and injects this context into a carefully crafted financial engineering prompt sent to the LLM.

(Action: Scroll down through the AI insights)
As you can see, the AI generates a comprehensive report rendered in formatted markdown:
1. Spending Analysis & Health: It breaks down the ratio of income spent on essentials versus discretionary expenses.
2. Overspending Detection: It flags categories where spending is disproportionately high.
3. Cost Optimization: It gives immediate, practical suggestions to cut down expenses.
4. Budget Allocation: It models a 50/30/20 budget framework customized to the user’s exact income level in Rupees.
5. Actionable Savings Steps: Clear steps to build an emergency fund and hit savings goals."
⏱️ 4:00 - 4:35 (35s)
5. Deployment with Ngrok
🖥️ On Screen: Terminal showing Flask running, and Ngrok public URL in browser.
"For testing, demonstration, and remote access, the project is configured for public deployment using Ngrok.

By tunneling our local Flask port through an authenticated Ngrok endpoint, the application is accessible from mobile devices or external testers anywhere with an HTTPS connection, making it simple to test cross-device responsiveness and real-world workflows."
